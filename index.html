My approach

BD is a process.

I approach business development as a process, not a single message. I start by understanding the target market, then find the right prospects and research what they need. I prioritise the best opportunities, start conversations that are relevant to them and keep every follow-up organised.

The work in this portfolio follows that path: two tools that collect and rank prospects, and an outreach method that starts from a real observation about the prospect’s business.

About

Hi, I’m Saher.

I’m a business development and lead generation professional based in Lahore. My day-to-day is finding businesses that could use a service, working out which ones deserve the most effort, and starting conversations that make sense for them.

I’ve built two browser extensions that organise prospecting, one for local businesses in Pakistan and one for international markets. I’ve also run diagnostic-led outreach during a contract role at a digital agency, where I studied each prospect’s website before writing to them.

I enjoy the research as much as the conversation. I’d rather tell you what a method has and hasn’t proven than make it sound bigger than it was.
